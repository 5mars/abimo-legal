Abimo Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Abimo ("the app", "we") is developed by Jeremy Cinq-Mars. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

What we collect

  • Account information. Your email address and a password, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Voice recordings. The voice notes you record in the app, so they can be transcribed and analyzed.
  • Transcriptions and analyses. The text transcribed from your recordings and the AI-generated analyses, scores, and action plans produced from them.
  • Usage analytics. Anonymous-by-default product events (screens viewed, features used, purchases) and crash reports, linked to a random user identifier. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

How your data is processed

  • Recordings, transcriptions, and analyses are stored on Supabase (hosted in the United States). Recordings live in a private storage bucket accessible only to your account.
  • To transcribe and analyze your ideas, your recording and its transcription are sent to OpenAI's API. OpenAI processes this data to produce the transcription and analysis and does not use API data to train its models.
  • Analytics and crash data are processed by Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics).
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We don't access your recordings except as needed to run the transcription and analysis you request.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. You can delete individual ideas at any time, and you can delete your entire account from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. Account deletion removes your recordings, transcriptions, analyses, action plans, and account record from our servers.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it by emailing jeremy@cinqmars.ca. If you are in the EU/EEA, UK, or a jurisdiction with similar data-protection laws (including Quebec's Law 25 and PIPEDA in Canada), you have the right to access, correct, export, and erase your personal data.

Children

Abimo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update this page and note the new date at the top.
