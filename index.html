The score is harsh on purpose.

Most idea tools tell you what you want to hear. Abimo's critic scores against evidence and a deliberately tough scale, so a 60 actually means something.

  • Evidence first. Live web research on your market, competitors and pricing — every number has a receipt.
  • Reasons you can act on. The bars tell you what would move the score, not just that it's low.
  • Re-taste later. Do the work, come back, and watch the number move.
